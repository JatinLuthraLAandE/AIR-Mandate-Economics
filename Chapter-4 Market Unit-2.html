Ch 4 Unit 2: Determination of Prices

Weightage: Core Micro Mechanics (~3–4 Marks) | Shifts in Demand & Supply Intersect

1. Determination of Equilibrium Price

Equilibrium: A state of balance where market forces of demand and supply operate in perfect harmony. The price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied is the Equilibrium Price ($P_e$), and the quantity is the Equilibrium Quantity ($Q_e$).

  • If $P > P_e \implies$ Excess Supply (Surplus): Sellers compete and bid down prices until equilibrium is restored.
  • If $P < P_e \implies$ Excess Demand (Shortage): Buyers compete and bid up prices until equilibrium is restored.

2. Simultaneous Shifts in Demand & Supply

What happens when BOTH demand and supply shift at the same time? The net impact on price and quantity depends on the relative magnitude of the shifts:

Case I: Demand & Supply Both Increase (Shift Right)

  • • If $\Delta D > \Delta S \implies$ Price Rises, Quantity Rises.
  • • If $\Delta D = \Delta S \implies$ Price remains Unchanged, Quantity Rises.
  • • If $\Delta D < \Delta S \implies$ Price Falls, Quantity Rises.
  • Rule: When both curves increase, Equilibrium Quantity ALWAYS increases! Price direction depends on who shifts more.

Case II: Demand & Supply Both Decrease (Shift Left)

  • • If $|\Delta D| > |\Delta S| \implies$ Price Falls, Quantity Falls.
  • • If $|\Delta D| = |\Delta S| \implies$ Price remains Unchanged, Quantity Falls.
  • • If $|\Delta D| < |\Delta S| \implies$ Price Rises, Quantity Falls.
  • Rule: When both curves decrease, Equilibrium Quantity ALWAYS falls!

Case III: Opposite Shifts (Demand Increases & Supply Decreases)

  • • Both forces pull price UPWARDS! Hence, Price ALWAYS Rises!
  • • Quantity change is indeterminate (Quantity rises if $\Delta D > \Delta S$; falls if $\Delta D < \Delta S$; unchanged if equal).

Case IV: Opposite Shifts (Demand Decreases & Supply Increases)

  • • Both forces pull price DOWNWARDS! Hence, Price ALWAYS Falls!
  • • Quantity change is indeterminate.
📌 Special Extreme Cases:
1. Perfect Inelastic Supply ($E_s = 0$): An increase in demand raises Price sharply; Quantity remains completely unchanged.
2. Perfect Elastic Supply ($E_s = \infty$): An increase in demand raises Quantity; Price remains completely unchanged.

🚨 ICAI Trap Zone & Mistake Tracker

Common Student Confusion ICAI Exam Standard & Fact
When Demand increases and Supply increases by the exact same proportion, Price rises. False! If $\Delta D = \Delta S$, the upward pull on price by demand is exactly offset by the downward push of supply. Price remains completely UNCHANGED, while Quantity increases!
When Demand increases and Supply decreases, Quantity always rises. False! Here, Price ALWAYS RISES. Quantity is indeterminate—it only rises if the increase in demand is larger than the drop in supply.

⚡ Rapid Revision & Mnemonic Matrix

🧠 Simultaneous Shift Rules: The "Q-Certain / P-Certain" Mnemonic

Same Direction Shifts ($D \uparrow S \uparrow$ or $D \downarrow S \downarrow$)

Quantity is CERTAIN (both $\uparrow \implies Q \uparrow$; both $\downarrow \implies Q \downarrow$). Price direction is indeterminate and depends on magnitude!

Opposite Direction Shifts ($D \uparrow S \downarrow$ or $D \downarrow S \uparrow$)

Price is CERTAIN ($D \uparrow S \downarrow \implies P \uparrow$; $D \downarrow S \uparrow \implies P \downarrow$). Quantity direction is indeterminate!
