Ch 5 Unit 1: National Income Accounting

Weightage: High Macro Pillar (~7–8 Marks) | Aggregates, Conversions & Measurement Methods

1. Definition & Fundamental Nature

National Income is the net money value of all final economic goods and services produced by normal residents of a country within an accounting year, plus net factor income from abroad.

  • It is a flow measure (measured per unit of time, normally one financial year), NOT a stock measure.
  • Only final goods and services are counted to avoid double counting; intermediate goods are excluded.
  • Transfer payments (scholarships, gifts, pensions of retired persons) are excluded because they do not correspond to any current productive factor service.
📌 The Three Golden Conversion Bridges:
1. Gross $\longleftrightarrow$ Net: $\text{Gross} - \text{Depreciation (CFC)} = \text{Net}$
2. Domestic $\longleftrightarrow$ National: $\text{Domestic} + \text{NFIA} = \text{National}$
3. Market Price $\longleftrightarrow$ Factor Cost: $\text{Market Price (MP)} - \text{NIT} = \text{Factor Cost (FC)}$
Where $\text{NIT} = \text{Indirect Taxes} - \text{Subsidies}$; $\text{NFIA} = \text{FIFA} - \text{FITA}$.

2. The 8 Core National Income Aggregates

  • $GDP_{MP}$ (Gross Domestic Product at Market Price): Market value of all final goods & services produced within domestic territory during a year, inclusive of depreciation.
  • $NDP_{MP}$: $GDP_{MP} - \text{Depreciation}$.
  • $GNP_{MP}$: $GDP_{MP} + \text{NFIA}$.
  • $NNP_{MP}$: $GNP_{MP} - \text{Depreciation}$.
  • $GDP_{FC}$: $GDP_{MP} - \text{NIT}$ (Sum of factor payments: Compensation of employees + Operating surplus + Mixed income + Depreciation).
  • $NDP_{FC}$ (Domestic Factor Income): $GDP_{FC} - \text{Depreciation}$.
  • $GNP_{FC}$: $GNP_{MP} - \text{NIT}$.
  • $NNP_{FC}$ (NATIONAL INCOME): $NDP_{FC} + \text{NFIA} = NNP_{MP} - \text{NIT}$. In economics, $NNP_{FC}$ is strictly called "National Income".

3. Three Methods of Measuring National Income

A. Product / Value Added Method (Output Method):

$\text{Gross Value Added (GVA at MP)} = \text{Value of Output} - \text{Intermediate Consumption}$

  • $\text{Value of Output} = \text{Sales} + \text{Change in Stock } (\text{Closing} - \text{Opening})$.
  • $\sum GVA_{MP} = GDP_{MP}$. Then deduct Depreciation and NIT, add NFIA to get $NNP_{FC}$.

B. Income Method (Factor Payment Method): Sums factor incomes earned by resident factor owners:

$NDP_{FC} = \text{Compensation of Employees (COE)} + \text{Operating Surplus (OS)} + \text{Mixed Income (MI)}$

  • COE: Wages & salaries (cash/kind) + Employer's contribution to social security schemes.
  • Operating Surplus: Rent + Royalty + Interest + Profit (Dividends + Undistributed Profits + Corporate Tax).
  • Mixed Income: Incomes of own-account workers and unincorporated enterprises (doctors, farmers, sole proprietors).

C. Expenditure Method: Sums final spending on GDP:

$GDP_{MP} = C + I + G + (X - M)$

  • $C$: Private Final Consumption Expenditure.
  • $I$: Gross Domestic Capital Formation (Gross Fixed Capital Formation + Change in Stock).
  • $G$: Government Final Consumption Expenditure.
  • $(X - M)$: Net Exports (Exports minus Imports).

4. Nominal GDP, Real GDP & The GDP Deflator

  • Nominal GDP (at Current Prices): Output valued at current year market prices ($P_1 \times Q_1$). Changes due to both price changes and quantity changes.
  • Real GDP (at Constant Prices): Output valued at base year prices ($P_0 \times Q_1$). Reflects true physical production growth.
  • GDP Deflator (Price Index): $$\text{GDP Deflator} = \frac{\text{Nominal GDP}}{\text{Real GDP}} \times 100$$

🚨 ICAI Trap Zone & Mistake Tracker

Common Student Error ICAI Standard & Fact
Sale of second-hand cars and commissions earned on them are both excluded. Careful distinction! The sale value of the second-hand car itself is excluded (counted in the year it was originally produced). However, the broker's commission or service fee is INCLUDED because it represents current productive service!
Transfer payments like old age pensions and unemployment allowances are included in Factor Income. False! Transfer payments are unearned one-way payments with no factor contribution; they are strictly EXCLUDED. However, retirement pensions (deferred wage) are INCLUDED under COE!
National Income is $GDP_{MP}$. False! In official economic terminology and ICAI exams, National Income strictly refers to $NNP_{FC}$ (Net National Product at Factor Cost).

🃏 3D Flip Flashcards

Mastered: 0 / 4
What is the difference between Old Age Pension and Retirement Pension in National Income?
Old Age Pension = Transfer Payment (EXCLUDED). Retirement Pension = Deferred Factor Payment (INCLUDED) as part of Compensation of Employees.
Calculate Real GDP if Nominal GDP = ₹6,600 crore and GDP Deflator = 110.
₹6,000 crore $\text{Real GDP} = \frac{\text{Nominal GDP}}{\text{Deflator}} \times 100 = \frac{6600}{110} \times 100 = 6000$.
Why is imputed rent of owner-occupied houses included in GDP?
It provides housing services with real market value! Even though no cash changes hands, the house produces productive housing utility comparable to rented property.
What is Mixed Income of the self-employed?
Combined return to labour, capital, land & entrepreneurship! Earned by unincorporated small proprietors (farmers, lawyers, shopkeepers) where wages, interest, rent, and profit cannot be separated.

🎯 Unit 1 Practice MCQs

Score: 0

1. National Income in economics strictly corresponds to:

Correct! In national accounting, National Income is identically defined as Net National Product at Factor Cost ($NNP_{FC}$).

2. Which of the following is INCLUDED in the estimation of National Income?

Correct! Brokerage/commission represents payment for current productive service and is always included. Windfall gains, second-hand assets, and scholarships are excluded.
